Arcane Undercroft

Game Synopsis

The Basement Wizard is trapped in a puzzle-filled dungeon, solve puzzles by swapping between four spells. Fire burns wood and rope, magic missile follows the cursor and can interact with buttons, force launches objects including ones too heavy for the player to move, and ice freezes water. Make it to the cake at the end of the game to confront the antagonist and win. The win screen depicts Gobby (from The Fell Gob), now reigning as a powerful king, battling Basement Wizard.
